Birth Family Tool, from Search Angels

What this installs
  - The Birth Family Tool program. Everything it needs is included,
    so there is nothing else to download or install.
  - An entry in "Add or remove programs", a Start menu entry and,
    if you want one, a desktop icon.

What it does not install
  - The optional public record files (about 28 GB). The tool works
    without them; they can be added later.

Space needed
  - About 60 MB for the program. The next screens show the exact
    figure for your computer.
  - Each case you work adds a few megabytes.

Your cases
  - Cases and trees are kept on this computer, separate from the
    program. Installing an update, or installing again to repair,
    does not change them.
  - If you uninstall, you are asked whether to delete them too.
